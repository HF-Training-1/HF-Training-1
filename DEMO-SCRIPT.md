# Five-minute demonstration

**Purpose:** HF Training is a barbering-focused academy workspace that can be tested alongside existing provision. It does not exchange data with ATS.

1. Learner: show a consultation, short/long hair descriptors, reflection and a fictional image.
2. Assessor: demonstrate independent criterion ticks and feedback, including returning work for improvement.
3. IQA: demonstrate a separate quality decision and the history of who did what.
4. Administrator: show separate VRQ, apprenticeship and VTCT enrolments. Explain that VTCT and apprenticeship content awaits configuration.
5. Show actual learning hours separately from assessment records. Discuss the evidence needed to measure time saved, learner engagement and assessment consistency during a pilot.

Do not claim funding approval, official awarding-body endorsement, proven learning outcomes, ATS integration or readiness for multiple independent colleges. This release supports one academy instance; separate college tenancy is future work.
