# Academy pilot acceptance

Use fictional records in a new demonstration directory.

1. Sign in as learner. Open unit 210. Enter a coded client reference, consultation, service, reflection and aftercare. Tick Short and Curly under practical ranges and a relevant draft criterion. Save and reopen; confirm the entries persist.
2. Upload a sample PDF or image without personal data. Submit. Confirm the form becomes read-only.
3. Sign out. As assessor, open the same learner, review evidence, select the demonstrated criteria, write feedback and record assessment.
4. As learner or assessor, add a second service for the unit. Confirm the original record and its history remain unchanged.
5. As IQA, sample the assessed record and record a decision. Check that learner and assessor accounts cannot do this action.
6. As admin, add another fictional learner to a different course. Confirm they cannot read the first learner or their files, or access the VRQ course when not enrolled.
7. Record separate placement and academy hours, approve them as assessor, and compare totals. Record attendance separately. Confirm absence requires zero minutes.
8. Record a fictional exam attempt, progress review, learner acknowledgement and resource. Check their course/learner visibility.
9. Export the learner record. It contains metadata and decisions; attached file bytes must be downloaded separately or retained via full backup.
10. Test browser navigation, keyboard use, desktop and phone layout, session expiry, returning work and concurrent stale edits.
11. Stop the app and back up; restore into a new data directory and verify evidence and records.
12. Before real use: approve the precise qualifications, official assessment mapping, data handling/consent process, host security, backup schedule and responsibility for support.

For a college/funder demonstration, describe this as an academy pilot. A course shell is not completed course content, and a successful demo is not certification or production acceptance.
